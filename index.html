Who We Are

Paver & Concrete Contractor Serving Pasco County, FL

Pallotta Pavers & Concrete LLC installs pavers, natural stone and poured concrete across Pasco County and northern Hillsborough County. We work in Wesley Chapel, Land O’ Lakes, Zephyrhills, Dade City, San Antonio, Spring Hill, Brooksville, Lutz and Odessa. We are licensed in Pasco County under C.C # LC-009985, insured and bonded, with more than 30 years in the trade.

Built for Central Florida Weather and Soil

Central Florida weather is hard on outdoor surfaces. Heavy summer rain, sandy soil and constant sun will find any shortcut in a base or a mix. We compact and drain every base properly, use high-grade materials, and place joints where they belong, so the work holds up year after year.

Clear Pricing and Straight Answers

You will know what is happening on your property from the first site visit to the final walkthrough. We quote after we look at your grade, drainage and soil, and we tell you if something on the plan will not work. The price we give you is the price you pay.

Most of our work comes from neighbors of past customers. If you want to see finished projects near you before deciding, ask and we will point you to them.
