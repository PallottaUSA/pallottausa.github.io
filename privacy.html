Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Pallotta Pavers & Concrete LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form, request a quote, or call us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, project address, and any details you choose to share about your project. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, prepare estimates, and communicate about ongoing or completed projects. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use our pages, such as which sections are viewed most often. This information is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to our social media pages (Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms once you leave our site.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request that we delete contact information you have previously submitted by reaching out via phone or email. We will honor such requests except where information must be retained for an active project or legal obligation.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, call 352-797-1716 or email us. Details are on our Contact page.
